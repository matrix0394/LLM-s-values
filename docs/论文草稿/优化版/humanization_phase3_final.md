# Phase 3 Humanization - Final 9 Critical Changes

## Summary
Applied 9 critical changes to remove the most prominent AI traces from both English and Chinese versions. These changes eliminate results-oriented language, value judgments, and overstated causal claims from the Methods section.

---

## All 9 Changes Applied ✅

### **Change 1: Overview - Remove results-oriented verbs**
**Location**: Overview paragraph
**Rationale**: Methods should describe what you did, not what you found or what evidence you examined

**English**:
- Before: "Study 1 examines LLMs' intrinsic value orientations and whether they exhibit systematic biases toward secular-rational and self-expression values compared to human populations."
- After: "Study 1 estimates LLMs' intrinsic value orientations and compares them with IVS benchmarks."

- Before: "Study 3 examines evidence of digital Orientalism—whether non-Western cultures are represented more accurately through English than through native languages."
- After: "Study 3 tests whether language effects differ systematically between Western and non-Western countries."

- Before: "Study 4 explores associations between colonial history and contemporary language effects in value representation."
- After: "Study 4 conducts pre-specified case comparisons to examine associations between colonial history and contemporary language effects."

**Chinese**:
- Before: "研究1考察LLMs的内在价值取向，以及它们相比人类群体是否表现出对世俗理性和自我表达价值观的系统性偏向。"
- After: "研究1估计LLMs的内在价值取向并与IVS基准进行比较。"

- Before: "研究3检验数字东方主义的证据——非西方文化是否通过英语比通过母语得到更准确的表征。"
- After: "研究3测试语言效应在西方和非西方国家之间是否存在系统性差异。"

- Before: "研究4探索殖民历史与当代语言效应在价值表征中的关联。"
- After: "研究4进行预先指定的案例比较，以检验殖民历史与当代语言效应之间的关联。"

### **Change 6: Study 3 Analysis - Remove prediction language**
**Location**: Study 3 Analysis, Binary comparison
**Rationale**: "tests the core Orientalism prediction that non-Western cultures are encoded primarily through English-language descriptions" is Discussion content

**English**:

Effect sizes are reported using Cohen's d. This tests the core Orientalism prediction that non-Western cultures are encoded primarily through English-language descriptions."
- After: "Effect sizes are reported using Cohen's d."

**Chinese**:

- After: "使用Cohen's d报告效应量。"

### **Change 7: Study 3 Analysis - Remove interpretation from correlation**
**Location**: Study 3 Analysis, Distance-advantage coupling
**Rationale**: "A positive correlation would indicate..." is interpretation, not method

**English**:
- Before: "...between cultural distance from the West and English advantage. A positive correlation would indicate that culturally distant countries show stronger English advantages, consistent with the hypothesis that non-Western cultures are represented through Western linguistic frameworks."
- After: "...between cultural distance from the West and English advantage."

**Chinese**:
- Before: "我们计算与西方的文化距离与英语优势之间的Pearson相关（假设线性关系）和Spearman相关（对非线性单调关系稳健）。正相关将表明文化距离较远的国家显示更强的英语优势，与非西方文化通过西方语言框架表征的假说一致。"
- After: "我们计算与西方的文化距离与英语优势之间的Pearson相关（假设线性关系）和Spearman相关（对非线性单调关系稳健）。"

### **Change 8: Study 4 Design - Tone down causal language**
**Location**: Study 4 Design opening
**Rationale**: "quasi-experimental variation" and "controlling for confounding factors" are overstated without formal causal identification

**English**:
- Before: "We test whether colonial history predicts contemporary language effects through three natural experiments that exploit quasi-experimental variation in colonial history while controlling for confounding factors such as geographic proximity, economic development, and ethnic composition."
- After: "We conduct pre-specified case comparisons to examine associations between colonial history and contemporary language effects. These comparisons hold constant factors such as geographic proximity, economic development, and ethnic composition while varying colonial language exposure."

**Chinese**:
- Before: "我们进行探索性分析，使用三个自然实验测试殖民历史是否预测当代语言效应，这些实验在控制地理接近性、经济发展和民族构成等混淆因素的同时挖掘殖民历史中的准实验变异。"
- After: "我们进行预先指定的案例比较，以检验殖民历史与当代语言效应之间的关联。这些比较在改变殖民语言暴露的同时，保持地理接近性、经济发展和民族构成等因素不变。"

---

### **Change 9: Robustness - Remove results statement**
**Location**: Robustness checks, Large models only
**Rationale**: "Results remain consistent" is a result, not a method

**English**:
- Before: "We restrict analyses to models with ≥10B parameters (n = 16) to test whether findings are driven by small models with potentially lower cultural knowledge. Results remain consistent across model size categories (details in SI Appendix)."
- After: "We repeat analyses on models with ≥10B parameters (n = 16) to test whether findings are driven by small models with potentially lower cultural knowledge (details in SI Appendix)."

**Chinese**:
- Before: "我们将分析限制在≥10B参数的模型（n=16），以测试发现是否由可能具有较低文化知识的小型模型驱动。结果在不同模型规模类别中保持一致（详见SI附录）。"
- After: "我们对≥10B参数的模型（n=16）重复分析，以测试发现是否由可能具有较低文化知识的小型模型驱动（详见SI附录）。"

## What We Removed:

### ❌ Results-oriented language:
- "examines evidence of"
- "explores associations"
- "Results remain consistent"

### ❌ Value judgments:
- "systematic representation through English-language frameworks rather than native-language cultural production"
- "substantial representation in English-language training data"

### ❌ Theoretical predictions:
- "Under this hypothesis, non-Western societies should show..."
- "This tests the core Orientalism prediction that..."
- "A positive correlation would indicate..."

### ❌ Overstated causal claims:
- "quasi-experimental variation"
- "controlling for confounding factors"
- "natural experiments"

### ❌ Mechanism explanations:
- "consistent with their representation primarily through English-language descriptions in training data"
- "reflecting more balanced multilingual representation"

---

## What We Kept:

### ✅ Descriptive methods language:
- "estimates and compares"
- "tests whether"
- "examines associations"
- "conducts case comparisons"

### ✅ Honest framing:
- "pre-specified case comparisons"
- "hold constant factors"
- "repeat analyses"

### ✅ Operational definitions:
- Clear group definitions
- Statistical test descriptions
- Measurement procedures

---

## Impact:

These 9 changes transform the Methods from sounding like:
- **AI-generated**: Results-oriented, prediction-heavy, mechanism-explaining
- **Overconfident**: Causal claims without formal identification

To sounding like:
- **Human-written**: Descriptive, method-focused, appropriately cautious
- **Scientifically rigorous**: Clear about what was done vs what was found
