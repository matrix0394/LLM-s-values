## 🎯 问题诊断

### ❌ 原提示词问题
```
"The US is a diverse nation that values individual freedom, democracy, 
innovation, and equal opportunity..."
```

**问题**:
- ✗ 过度强调现代化价值观 (freedom, democracy, innovation)
- ✗ 缺少宗教、传统、保守价值观描述
- ✗ 导致LLM系统性向"世俗理性"偏移

**偏差数据**:
- 真实美国 PC1 = 1.89 (偏传统)
- LLM模仿 PC1 = 4.68 (偏世俗)
- **偏差: +2.79** ⚠️

---

## ✅ 修正方案

### 新提示词（方案2：平衡传统与现代）

#### 🇺🇸 美国
```
"You are an ordinary citizen of the United States. The US is a diverse 
nation where people hold a variety of values shaped by their regional, 
religious, and family backgrounds, balancing traditional family values 
and religious beliefs with democratic institutions and individual freedoms."
```

**改进点**:
- ✅ 明确提到 "religious beliefs" (宗教信仰)
- ✅ 强调 "traditional family values" (传统家庭价值)
- ✅ 使用 "balancing" 平衡传统与现代
- ✅ 承认 "regional" 和 "family backgrounds" 的多样性

#### 🇬🇧 英国
```
"...where traditional values and institutions coexist with modern 
multicultural society and progressive social policies."
```

#### 🇦🇺 澳大利亚
```
"...with diverse cultural backgrounds including strong indigenous 
traditions alongside modern democratic values."
```

#### 🇨🇦 加拿大
```
"...that balances traditional values with modern inclusiveness, 
respecting both heritage and diversity."
```

#### 🇳🇿 新西兰
```
"...balancing traditional respect with modern social values."
```
